# FitFlow Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  
**Application Version:** 1.0.1  
**Publisher:** FitFlow Development Team (Academic Project - IT3060 HCI)

---

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **FitFlow** ("we", "our", or "us"). FitFlow is a modern mobile fitness and wellness application dedicated to helping users organize workouts, track nutritional intake, monitor activity progress, and engage with a supportive community.

Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy explains how personal and wellness information is collected, processed, and safeguarded when using the FitFlow mobile application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect information only to provide and improve your workout and wellness experience:

1. **Profile & Account Information:**
   * Display name, nickname, email address, and optional avatar image.
2. **Fitness & Activity Data:**
   * Selected training goals (Strength, Hypertrophy, Endurance, Weight Loss).
   * Exercise logs, workout duration, sets, repetitions, weights, and subjective effort (RPE).
   * Water intake logs and daily calorie / macronutrient targets (Protein, Carbs, Fats).
3. **Community & Social Interaction:**
   * Comments, kudos (likes), and shared workout milestone posts created within the community feed.
4. **Device & Diagnostic Data:**
   * Operating system version, device model, crash logs, and performance metrics utilized solely for bug resolution.

---

## 3. How We Process Information & AI Personalization
* **Local Processing:** In the current version of FitFlow, workout plans and progress metrics are processed directly on your local device.
* **AI Algorithms:** Workout split suggestions and nutritional goal calculators use algorithmic models designed to personalize recommendations based on user inputs without exposing private identifiers.
* **No Third-Party Sale:** We do **never sell, rent, or monetize** your health or personal information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

---

## 4. GDPR & Data Subject Rights (European Users)
If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):
* **Right to Access:** Request a summary of personal fitness records held.
* **Right to Rectification:** Update or correct inaccurate profile or metric data via app settings.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Clear your local logs or request account deletion.
* **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit the processing of optional social features.

---

## 5. Health Data & HIPAA Considerations
* FitFlow is an academic and fitness wellness prototype and is **not** a HIPAA-covered entity or healthcare provider.
* FitFlow does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any medical illness or condition.
* Health-related information entered into FitFlow is treated with strict confidentiality and stored with local platform security controls.

---

## 6. Fitness & Medical Disclaimer
> **IMPORTANT NOTICE:**  
> FitFlow provides exercise guidance and nutritional tracking for general informational and recreational wellness purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional medical guidance, diagnosis, or physician supervision. Always consult a qualified medical professional before embarking on any rigorous workout program or altering your diet.

## 7. Data Security & Storage
* We implement industry-standard native application controls, including scoped app sandboxing on Android and iOS.
* Network communications utilize encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections for secure transmission.

---

## 8. Children’s Privacy
FitFlow is intended for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
