FitFlow Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 1.0.1

Health Disclaimer: FitFlow is designed solely for fitness tracking and recreational wellness guidance. It is not a medical diagnostics or healthcare delivery application. Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare physician before starting any intense workout or nutritional regimen.

1. Overview & Commitment

FitFlow ("we", "our") is dedicated to protecting your personal health and activity data. This policy outlines how information is gathered, managed, and safeguarded when using the FitFlow mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile: Display name, email, profile photo, and fitness experience level.
  • Workout Logs & Activity: Selected split routines, exercise repetitions, lifted weights, and session durations.
  • Nutrition & Water: Calorie goals, daily macronutrients (protein, carbs, fats), and hydration counters.
  • Social Interactions: Public comments, kudos, and community workout posts.
  • Diagnostics: Crash diagnostics and technical performance logs to fix app bugs.

3. Local Processing & AI Recommendations

Personalization and workout split algorithms operate using sanitized local inputs. Your health and personal identifiers are never sold, rented, or distributed to advertisers.

4. GDPR & User Rights

Under GDPR, users hold the right to access, rectify, and permanently delete their stored fitness logs. Account deletion or local reset options are accessible directly in the mobile application settings.

5. Security Standards

We leverage native mobile sandboxing, TLS encryption protocols, and zero unauthorized external data sharing to ensure all fitness logs stay safe.

6. Contact Us

For inquiries, support, or privacy questions, email us at support@fitflow.app.
